A Field Guide for Writers

Essential Tools of Analysis

Every strong analysis is carried by five instruments. Passion orients the writer — the rest are the tools they use to chart, build, and voice what they see.

N
Field Notes

Ideas

The concepts and issues the writer explores.

Ideas encompass the writer's thesis, claims, evidence, and reasoning — including the counterclaims a writer takes seriously enough to answer.

W
Credentials

Authority

The writer's know-how for exploring a text.

Authority speaks to a writer's content knowledge, the connections they draw between the text, other texts, and ideas, their own humanity and voice.

✦
Compass

Passion

The writer's compass — and the center every other tool takes its bearing from.

Passion is a wholehearted investment in the text — deeper than admiration, full of conviction that it matters. The writer cares about what they're saying.

E
Map

Structure

The maps a writer uses to chart their course.

Structure includes the writer's focus, form, and paragraphing — how they lead and conclude, and the visual structure tools they use.

S
Toolkit

Style

The writer's choices in craft.

Style covers word choice, detail, and syntax, literary and rhetorical technique, figurative language, and the deliberate use of grammar, punctuation and convention.

Adapted from Beyond Literary Analysis: Teaching Students to Write with Passion and Authority About Any Text by Allison Marchetti & Rebekah O'Dell (p. 35), and Poetry as Persuasion by Carl Dennis (p. 17).
