Discussion Questions

The Wage Standard — for use during class discussion

Warm-up

Why has America become wealthier while many workers still struggle?

Activity: Who Gets the Raise?

Your company earned an extra $1,000,000 this year. How should it be distributed?

  • A. Give most of it to executives.
  • B. Give identical raises to everyone.
  • C. Give larger raises to low-wage workers.
  • Which option is most fair?
  • Which option reduces poverty?
  • Which option improves motivation?
Applying to Poverty

Should workers receive a larger share of economic growth? Why or why not?

Closing Reflection

What is one change you would make to reduce poverty through wages?
